# PLUTO — brand

*Owner text, 2026-10-31. Tagline in the app and READMEs: "Small companion. Big universe."*

### Your personal productivity ecosystem

**THE CORE WORLD**

Pluto is a connected personal productivity ecosystem that brings your everyday digital life together in one place.

Inspired by the idea of a small world with its own universe of possibilities, Pluto combines a playful desktop companion with smart tools that help you stay focused, organized, and productive.

## THE PLUTO ECOSYSTEM

Each capability is another part of the Pluto world.

| PLUTO | PURPOSE |
|---|---|
| **FOCUS** | Attention |
| **TRACK** | Time |
| **NOTES** | Knowledge |
| **PROJECTS** | Work |
| **AI** | Intelligence |
| **COMPANION** | Character |

## THE BRAND IDEA

A connected ecosystem of productivity features.

Pluto may occupy only a small space on your desktop, but it brings together the tools, intelligence, and personality that support your entire day.

- **Small:** Lightweight and always within reach.
- **Smart:** Intelligent features that simplify everyday work.
- **Connected:** One ecosystem where all capabilities work together.
- **Personal:** A playful character that gives the ecosystem its identity.

## BRAND IDENTITY

Pluto is more than a desktop companion. It is a little world of connected productivity tools, brought together through one character and one unified experience.

**PLUTO — Your personal productivity ecosystem**
